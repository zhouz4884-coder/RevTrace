# Metric and evidence dictionary

- Primary Audio Goodput: every requested fine frontier is weighted by its newly
  covered audio; actual local Consumer valid-visible time<=2s and Owner durable
  install<=8s, divided by complete measured serving/drain/Consumer wall. The
  Consumer commit addendum substitutes actual certified-Consumer application.
  Neither clock is a network/browser latency or production-traffic estimate.
- Canonical terminal WER/CER use fixed full-source references and integer edit
  counts. No claim that provisional text is correct, stable or equivalent to
  Canonical text; retractions/invalidations are separately retained.
- GPU J/audio-s is independently trapezoid-integrated for both physical GPUs
  over the exact serving window, excluding sampling padding/model setup.
  Initial pilot1's inadequate boundary coverage is marked, not extrapolated
  into a strict comparison. Host CPU energy is not measured.
- Timed commit-path fsync counts are observed WAL fsync events; initial genesis,
  directory setup and model initialization are outside that counter. Their
  actual time is not silently removed from the serving wall where it occurs.
- Candidate wait is max(0,actual candidate Owner-receive minus target eligibility)
  summed over verified rows. The raw legacy column is unfortunately named
  candidate_wait_before_eligibility_seconds; its formula is AFTER eligibility.
  GROUPS uses the corrected alias candidate_wait_after_target_eligible_s.
  Release-to-enqueue age additionally includes Parent/queue delay, not just wait.
- Obsolete row latency sums overlap and are NOT GPU seconds. Generated-token
  counts and associated physical-call envelopes are measured separately. A
  mixed physical call touching an obsolete row is not exclusive wasted work;
  its envelope is not a counterfactual amount that could have been saved.
- CES descendant age/conditional-render compatibility are cost predictors, not
  hardware attestation, new-parent GPU generation or certain invalidation.
- Repeated runs and repeated state contexts are not additional acoustic sources.
  Training: development16 only; test pool: previously independent16 sources,
  excluded from CES fitting. Each method's changed actual batch/history gets
  its own input, admission, WAL, Consumer and quality audit.
- Main Mixed uncertainty:3 fresh-process repeats with16 source clusters per
  pool, conditional on observed schedules. Clean/high each1 repeat are descriptive.
  Pointwise95% and conservative simultaneous99.84375% bands are distinct.
  A result below predeclared substantive gates is not proof of zero utility.
- CPU fixtures/decision replay, operator/oracle diagnostic work, actual pilot
  serving and frozen formal serving are distinct evidence tiers. Oracle extra
  calls are paid and logged, not included as regular deployment speed numbers.
