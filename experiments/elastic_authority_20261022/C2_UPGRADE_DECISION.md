# C2 Elastic Upgrade Decision

**NO_GO_C2_ELASTIC_ALGORITHM_UPGRADE.** Primary, Commit-P99 secondary and energy secondary all fail the predeclared success rules. Quality noninferiority passes. Stop additional GPU experiments, leave the paper unchanged and retain the original DGFT contribution structure.

Deadline-only's +6.62% Goodput point vs the best fixed has CI [−7.44%,27.90%]. It is not a substitute statistically established success. All formal whole-run P99s miss 2s/8s; partial-goodput must not be called capacity/SLO pass.

## Failure taxonomy and controls

- **Performance-hypothesis failure:** the frozen Joint rule does not deliver the required advantage. Same input, four repeats, common carrier/protection/resources and real independently legal fixed/simple histories prevent a weak-baseline escape.
- **Not a discovered admission defect:** no new violation was found in the formal evidence, and protected primitives did not change. This does not prove absence of every possible bug.
- **Not established as a numerical/model failure:** no controlled precision defect was demonstrated. Different rho/history may legitimately change outputs. A small WER point improvement does not explain away worse performance.
- **Pre-GPU engineering defects:** sample guard terminal handling was fixed before GPU runs (10 failed / 138 passed →148 passed); incomplete legacy pytest dependencies and two packaging metadata issues are retained in `FAILURE_AND_REVISION_HISTORY.md`.
- **Legal oracle boundary:** each protected policy has its own actual FP32 authority generation and independent ledger/Render replay. No global optimal dynamic oracle was constructed; timing-only screens are explicitly not legal model-output oracles.

## What remains useful for the paper later

The safety obligations, rho2 operating point, and workload-dependent crossover extend the evaluated design space of C2. The original contribution is still fixed-contract rate separation plus causal dual-time-line coordination, not this unsuccessful scheduler. C1 E1/R5, A/V0/V1 NO-GO boundaries, and C3's recovery-clock tiers remain in the prior archives. No manuscript change has been made or authorized in this study.

## Stop rationale

The user explicitly conditioned an algorithm upgrade on repeatable superiority over strong fixed and simple policies. All frozen success paths have been evaluated and fail. Continuing to tune thresholds, cherry-pick loads or add models until a positive result would invalidate this study. A future Goodput-aware overload rule would require a new predeclared hypothesis; it is not implemented or claimed here.
