   rule can realize different target jobs after scheduling changes. A changed
   batch width may expose peers but does not prove their outputs changed. Report
   both actual job dosing and these unresolved associations,not a single cause.

## Decision discipline

Keep separate outcomes for interface feasibility,safety tests,reproducible shared
system gain,new algorithm increment,and the conditional rescue route. An evidenced
NO-GO on one increment does not erase original C1/C2/C3 or prove the topic exhausted.
Conversely,a practical win cannot substitute for a missing independent mechanism.
No post-outcome source/model/precision/deadline tuning is permitted in the frozen
matrix. No manuscript language is being changed in this phase.
