readiness denominators; do not select by margin or final success. C1 is Fine
same-target candidate, C2 last native private candidate at the Coarse target,
C3 a separately paid root-aligned FP16 draft. Formal fixed-state V1 generation

outcomes; exploratory/debug records are never pooled into the formal set.

All candidate tokens 1..m, EOS/allowed stop or exact max-token termination,
padding and trusted-context projection must be certified with FP32. Every
accepted row must equal an independently generated FP32 full-token and state
oracle in the matched input/batch layout, including same-input repeats.
Rejects pay complete FP32 fallback cost; no KV reuse is implemented.

Strong verifier control (fixed before V1 GPU): after all Z tokens and stopping
are verified under the trusted FP32 target context, derive Phi from that
trusted context and Z. Do not install untrusted offered P. Report offered-
pair acceptance separately; a mismatch of offered P is not used to reject a
legally reconstructed verified Z/P pair. Compare the returned reconstructed
P with the independent FP32 oracle. This is adapter reconstruction after
selection, not a new KV reuse or unverified reconstruction shortcut.
FP32 oracle-as-candidate positive controls and their same-input re-scores
validate position/mask/stop behavior independently of FP16 disagreement.

V2 request gate (all conjunctive): no false accept; repeat oracle stable;
eligible-and-ready fraction >=25% on live opportunities; >=10% inclusive
cost saving after extra drafts, verification, full fallback and capacity
occupancy; measured/supported commit-tail risk <=5% versus independent
authority and no reintroduced candidate wait. A fixed-state mean cost alone
cannot certify the tail. Missing tail evidence => NO_GO_ONLINE_SELECTOR,
not permission to implement V2. Passing all gates permits a request, not
automatic unapproved new KV-reuse or another online architecture.
