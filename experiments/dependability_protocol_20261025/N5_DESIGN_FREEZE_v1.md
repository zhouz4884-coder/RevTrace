# N5 bounded development protocol, prior to any new GPU result

ExperimentID REVTRACE-CDE-N5-20261026. Extends, does not replace, P0–P5 and the
user's additive contract. N1 is complete/audited and must not be rerun.

## Fixed mechanism and measurable obligations

Same independent FP32 Authority, FP16 Proposer, two RTX3090s, fine2s, fixed rho1
for Fine/rho3 for all coarse policies, shared Row-complete/D1/A/B/C/D/WAL and
full two_lane Consumer. No evidence-selector, rho optimization, KV reuse, DAG
grafting or deadline bypass. The controller never supplies selected canonical P.

O=offered released prefix, A=durably accepted prefix, C=canonical durable prefix;
V=actual applied legal provisional view. Preserve0<=C<=A<=O and sum(R-C)<=B,
where R reserves complete authority intervals. Multiple intervals may be
buffered when actual offered data and budget permit; safe baselines are not
artificially forced to one interval of private lead. Grouped input snapshots
persist A/R/EOF/rejections atomically before any input ACK or dispatch eligibility.
The immutable prepared audio/manifest must remain available: this is arrival
replay, not a deployed durable network broker. Conventional WAL technique is
credited; shared accounting is not attributed as exclusive CDE algorithm novelty.

The observed policy inputs are actual queues/lease ownership, committed/accepted
debt, and immutable ACKs emitted after actual local Consumer application. Full
CDE additionally uses unobserved-by-current-view accepted debt; no future token
length, ground-truth fault, target payload or WER enters the rule. Idle/missing
metrics are not oracle completion. CERTIFIED-ONLY withdraws provisional display
by typed control, drains authority work and never cancels an active GPU lease.
Only **unissued** expired private targets may be explicitly retired. Their audio
stays in canonical/input obligations and all offered-frontier denominators.

Recovery validates both logs and actual accepted byte-prefix hashes, reconciles
canonical C from the canonical WAL, restores reservations/rejections and fences
old ACKs. The original offered clock/deadlines persist. New Consumer application,
not owner load or a stale visible fact, ends recovery. The current prototype is
single-owner, single-host process restart, successful intact storage, finite
replay input. Permanent source disappearance and storage loss do not get a
bounded availability promise.

## Baselines, fixed pre-pilot controller parameters

Methods: fine_fair, dgft_original, standard_fair, queue_expiry, cde_age, cde_full.
An additional standard_cde transfer positive control can be run in confirmation;
it receives the same CDE rules, not denied a capability to manufacture novelty.
Fine/Standard/fair-queue controls share the same input-credit budget and ordinary
deadline-expired private retirement. dgft_original retains original scheduling
with common observation/input accounting but an explicitly nonbinding credit
budget; it is not the sole comparator for a CDE advantage.

Parameters before pilot: B=96 audio-s, warning4s/critical8s/clear2s,
DisplaySLO2s/CommitSLO8s, ordinary queue threshold8, reject only wholly unaccepted
new sessions after8s waiting,5ms owner tick. All actual input/WAL/fsync/IPC and
already issued obsolete computation is charged. CDE-age is the ablation of
queue/lease/valid-view conditional control, not a cheaper model or weaker safety.

## Data and initial bounded pilot

Development sources are the existing metadata-ordered PhaseD16 full English
utterances. This is reused development, not unseen validation. Freeze exact
manifest/schedule/audio hashes before execution. Initial pilot is four cells:
fine_fair and cde_full at nonsaturated4 and sustained16(uniform0.25s stagger).
Its purpose is live-path legality, observed control actions and full cost/input
audit, not statistical superiority. Then development strong-baseline comparison
and a real process-kill recovery case if this gate passes.

Independent validation will use a new, source-disjoint metadata-selected pool,
frozen **before** any validation generation. Do not search for bad outputs or
reuse a held-out pool to choose parameters. Formal sources, exact workloads,
gate/statistical definitions and all methods are frozen after development audit,
not silently inferred from this pilot. No formal performance success yet.

Required workload classes in final matrix: nonsaturated, near capacity, burst,
finite sustained overload, low->high->low return-to-normal, and controlled
process-fault/recovery. Original offered times remain in every denominator,
including rejected, unfinished and downtime input. It is not an unlimited soak.

## Metrics and gates

Primary service metrics are independently reconstructed over every originally
offered audio frontier, not legacy event-only child P99. Keep actual certified
Consumer coverage/offered separate from owner C, valid provisional coverage,
explicit rejection/unfinished counts, P50/P95/P99 (unserved=inf), entire-run
dual-P99, paired AudioGoodput, WER/CER over all offered references and completed
subset, debt/queue peak/AUC, real GPU energy, physical batches, CPU and both WALs.
Old startup/model-load and new certified-Consumer clocks remain distinct.

Pilot gate: no unauthorized canonical admission, no model reads beyond actually
durable A or released O, no phantom progress after rejection, exact input/render/
WAL/row lease audits, persistent original clock, all offered outcomes accounted.
Any failed cell/raw log is retained; no automatic retuning/retry from timeout.
Code/test-fixture errors are repaired with a versioned same-input control.

Only repeatable independent reliable-service/performance benefit beyond ordinary
safe queue/expiry/fair-limit controls promotes a new C2/C3 mechanism. A positive
against unlimited original DGFT alone is insufficient. Equal ordinary control
means specification/engineering completion, not exclusive algorithm victory.
No fabricated2sSLOpass, new general theorem, natural prevalence or acceptance
probability. Final gates must include quality/coverage so rejection cannot buy a
false energy/latency win.
