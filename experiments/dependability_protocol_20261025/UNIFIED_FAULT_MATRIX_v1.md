C1=trusted feedback write permission; C2=dual-timeline legal visibility and honest
degradation; C3=durable recoverable admission plus accepted-input obligations.
Conditional specifications/guard witnesses can support protocol contributions.
New policy-performance claims require independent replicated benefit over full
safe controls, including Standard with identical controller opportunity. If
these are equal, the contribution is a portable application contract and tested
coordination obligations, not a novel universal transaction primitive or an
exclusive algorithm win. Final manuscript awaits the remaining research gates.
