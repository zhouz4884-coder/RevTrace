## What answers the scientific question

Correct Canonical history leaves three holes unless separately addressed:
(1)a freshly delivered old Private computation may not describe a causal
continuation of the newroot; (2)overload can delay or suppress legitimate views
while event-filtered metrics silently omit offered frontiers; (3)durable state
replay alone neither preserves accepted audio obligations nor immediately
restores their actual visible facts. These are different observable failures,
not synonyms for state corruption. The application protocol gives each a typed
fact and a checkable transition; N1 and N5 supply distinct real measurements.

The additional value is **not** that conventional components cannot implement
it. FullStandard does implement it and maywin performance. The publishable
claim is the problem/contract, complete causal-visible coordination, explicit
debt/recovery obligations, conditional arguments, and falsifiable fault/cost
evidence. Whether this systems increment is sufficiently novel for DSN remains
a real reviewer decision; no numerical result proves acceptance probability.
