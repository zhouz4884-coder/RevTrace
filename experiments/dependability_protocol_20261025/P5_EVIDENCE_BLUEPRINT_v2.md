## Three preserved contributions, with object/boundary/guarantee/cost

### C1 Trusted feedback-state admission

Object:model-generated projection P. Boundary:installation as a future Canonical
Parent. Guarantee:under trusted reference execution/caller/adapter assumptions,
complete selection evidence plus Parent/audio/contract/stop/Phi bindings authorizes
the actual selected transition. Consistency alone is not selection evidence.
Cost:trusted generation or complete correct-context verification with real paid
fallback; neither path is uniquely necessary by naming an Authority role.

Direct evidence:E1/online72, bounded V1 checker controls/cost, conventional full
predicate composition, original fuzzer. Actual processed-input audit is an
observation under a trusted Worker, not hardware attestation. Certificates do
not certify transcript truth or completeness. Coherent attack GPU evidence must
remain pending until actual runs/audits, not copied from the CPU role fixture.

### C2 Dual-timeline causal coordination

Object:frozen private computations and actual provisional/certified views.
Boundary:result acceptance and later Consumer application after Canonical change.
Guarantee:owner freshness prevents newly obsolete publication; exact same-boundary
projection/full-chain compatibility determines retained private views. Typed
publication does not make a private suffix certified or permit string grafting.
Cost/utility:rate and display waiting are separately measurable levers, with
workload/quality/latency limits; shared Row-complete benefits belong to all methods.

Direct evidence:N1 actual unchanged normalized Canonical histories, explicit
actual-view witness and strong protected controls; PhaseD chain/retention and
repairedT1 factorial; finite model/counterexamples. Current32-speaker normal105
provides modern same-contract full-offered service boundaries, not a universal
dominance result. Full Standard may match/win and must remain prominent.

CDE five-mode/input-credit implementation is a tested service-contract extension,
but its extra feedback control did not establish independent performance value
against safe ordinary controls. Use its state/metric semantics and measured
negative tradeoffs only at their evidence tier. Do not upgrade it to the new
central algorithm contribution. Ordinary queue/admission/degradation have
SEDA/USITS precedent. Terminal rejection has CPU evidence but was0in normal105;
do not silently call that branch GPU-covered.

### C3 Durable recoverable selected and accepted obligations

Object:selected transitions/receipts, trusted rendered facts and accepted-input
prefix debt. Boundary:successful sync before receipt/eligibility and crash→new
owner/Consumer epoch. Guarantee:under intact available storage/audio, replay
restores selected ancestry and duplicate identities; input receipt recovery
reconciles actual C within A; certified facts can be reapplied without relabeling
old Private as new certified coverage. Cost:real sync/bytes/CPU and cold service
recovery, with distinct clocks and no cross-fault-domain RTO substitution.

Direct evidence:original/PhaseD/Row WAL/SIGKILL; new2development process-group
recoveries with all gates;21formal independent-source recoveries still pending.

exactly-once RPC/display primitive, arbitrary host/power failure or bounded
network-client RTO is claimed. Mode labels do not override admission safety.
