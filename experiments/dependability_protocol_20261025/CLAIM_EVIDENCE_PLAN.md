# P4/P5 claim-evidence design — no evidence laundering

This is a candidate argument, not a claim that P0–P5 or formal N1 are complete.

## Central contribution

RevTrace specifies and implements distinct permissions for a revisable output
and a model-generated state transition. The runtime binds the selected feedback
projection to committed ancestry and input progress, coordinates independently
advancing private/display and authority timelines, and makes admitted facts and
their certified publication recoverable. Ordinary validation, fencing and WAL
can implement this specification: full Standard is an explicit positive control.

## First-two-page and 11-technical-page design

Page1: concrete future-use boundary and measured controlled consequence; central
contract; three contributions tied to evidence, not a long list of new names.
Page2: minimal schedule example with both owner-publication and actual-Consumer
outcomes, followed by threat/assumption table. Do not assume N1 illegal-view count.
Pages3–5: state machine, admission predicate, exact-chain continuity, WAL/typed
publication, separate conditional invariants. Avoid introducing unexplained
certificate machinery before the motivating failure.
Pages6–9: necessity/fault matrix, repaired two-lever factor, strong alternatives,
capacity/quality/cost including losses, real recovery with distinct clocks.
Pages10–11: relation to verified speculation/stream processing/recovery, limits
and conclusion. References separate; mandatory AI Tool Usage outside technical
count as the official CFP allows. No font/margin tricks to fit pages.
