Abstract—Feedback-driven inference can retract timely output
whose computation remains valid. RevTrace coordinates three
decisions: complete-input eligibility, original selection authority,
and support for the Consumer’s whole hypothesis. It retains
eligible calls without endorsing their predictions and preserves
accepted results across producer failure. We give sufficient
retention conditions and a conditional selected-trace refinement
argument. In a predeclared 24-speaker stratum, computation
retention increases frontier withdrawals from 12 to 17; joint
retention yields zero. In a matched four-speaker native correction
study, offered audio coverage meeting both delivery deadlines rises
from 45.2% to 99.2% with unchanged selected histories. Wholerun passes increase from zero with conservative execution to six
of eight with RevTrace. Controlled producer failures preserve
already received complete results when their inputs match and
reject them when they do not.

C. [EL] Retaining computation: R
The core Qwen guard conservatively implements Proposition 1. On installation, it requires a complete Private node at
exactly F with full feedback tokens PC , then checks frozen
inputs and frontiers along complete successors to the actual tip.
An intermediate match is insufficient. An in-flight successor
must be the unique tracked task, consume that tip, and target
audio beyond F .
Success retains the branch without changing job IDs, birth
versions, inputs, or results; otherwise the Owner fences it and
rebuilds from an allowed state. Boundary-node and unique-slot
requirements restrict this sufficient guard, not every valid reuse
scheme.

IV. E XECUTION P ROTOCOL
A. Complete results and physical ownership
In the core runtime, dispatch binds session, incarnation, job,
input, target, and contract. A row returns either a complete
result with re-derived state/text or cancellation without partial
state. Complete rows can be installed and consumed before
their batch finishes. Only all terminal rows and device synchronization authorize BatchDrained and executor reuse. The
native process lifecycle below has separate evidence.
B. Covered work and safe-point exit: K
For the tested use model, K requests exit when the installed
Canonical frontier covers a proposal’s target and it has no
remaining declared use. This differs from R: valid work
beyond the new root can remain useful. Workers observe
CancelRequest at decode boundaries; completion can win,
but each row has one terminal outcome. Static batches are not
compacted or refilled; masked costs and drain delays remain
measured.
