Abstract—Feedback-driven inference can retract timely output
whose computation remains valid. RevTrace coordinates three
decisions: complete-input eligibility, original selection authority,
and support for the Consumer’s whole hypothesis. It retains
eligible calls without endorsing their predictions and preserves
accepted results across producer failure. We give sufficient
retention conditions and a conditional selected-trace refinement
argument. In a predeclared 24-speaker stratum, computation
retention increases frontier withdrawals from 12 to 17; joint
retention yields zero. In a matched four-speaker native correction
study, offered audio coverage meeting both delivery deadlines
rises from 45.2% to 99.2% with unchanged selected histories.
Whole-run passes increase from zero with conservative execution
to six of eight with RevTrace. No illegal installation occurs among
3,976 audited transitions in fault and sustained-state tests; cold
recovery and no-reset quality failures delimit the service claim.

II. S YSTEM AND F EEDBACK C ONTRACT
A. System and Failure Model
Each session has an Owner serializing selected transitions,
workers executing frozen calls, and an actual Consumer
applying selected facts and complete provisional hypotheses.
Durable storage records the selected prefix. A Supervisor
monitors the processes and preserves the accepted-input ledger.
We assume one trusted Owner per session, protected selection, immutable audio and model configuration, unambiguous
encoding, collision-resistant digests, and storage honoring
successful synchronization. Used calls must faithfully consume
their frozen inputs. Host receipts bind API objects, not GPU
arithmetic; independent Private and Authority floating-point
executions need not agree.
The interface checks identity, completeness, derived state,
and ancestry. Native resilience supports worker or Owner-group
fail-stop on an intact filesystem; Supervisor, accepted-input
ledger, actual Consumer, power observer, and synchronized storage survive. Their original input clocks continue through restart.
Supervisor/Consumer failure, host power loss, compromised
Owner or Authority, Byzantine code, persistent corruption, and
lost storage are excluded. Controlled wrong-input injections in
Section V intentionally violate normal Private input fidelity;
we use them to study containment and executor release, not as
examples covered by the lawful-continuation proposition.
B. Feedback creates an execution dependency
An immutable audio stream supplies a protected selection
path producing Canonical facts and a faster Private path
computing and displaying provisional hypotheses. Selection
can change the next call’s input: StreamCorrect feeds its
chosen correction into subsequent ASR [1]. We coordinate
this dependency as the paths advance asynchronously.

C. [EL] Retaining computation: R
The core Qwen guard conservatively implements Proposition 1. On installation, it requires a complete Private node at
exactly F with full feedback tokens PC , then checks frozen
inputs and frontiers along complete successors to the actual tip.
An intermediate match is insufficient. An in-flight successor
must be the unique tracked task, consume that tip, and target
audio beyond F .
Success retains the branch without changing job IDs, birth
versions, inputs, or results; otherwise the Owner fences it and
rebuilds from an allowed state. Boundary-node and unique-slot
requirements restrict this sufficient guard, not every valid reuse
scheme.
D. [SUP] Retaining an actual view: S
A snapshot Consumer can replace a valid ahead view with
a shorter certified snapshot despite computation retention.
RevTrace maintains separate certified and provisional wholehypothesis lanes, without an uncertified suffix splice or certifiedprefix status for retained views.
For a Canonical application, S checks the complete visible
node’s input directly against (F, PC ) or follows complete
published ancestors to that boundary. Missing, cyclic, or
ambiguous ancestry is rejected. Unlike R’s conservative guard,
a directly rooted published call needs no extra Private boundary
node.
Before actual application and its receipt, the kernel checks the
preview against the durable root, registered results, dependency
path, and bound text/frontier. A caller-supplied compatibility
flag cannot replace these records.
Proposition 2 (Continuity at a compatible application). Immediately before an authorized Canonical application, let the
visible whole provisional view h be ahead of its new frontier
F . If S proves C ⇝ h and no independent withdrawal mode
is invoked, that application advances the certified lane and
preserves h unchanged.
Proof. The authorized compatible-and-ahead branch installs C
in the certified lane while retaining h’s node, text, and frontier.
That application therefore withdraws none of h.
Incompatible updates must withdraw the view, and later valid
results can revise it. This is a per-application property, not
global monotonicity. Snapshot consumption and conservative
recomputation can be safe; the four-arm study measures the
extra progress and cost of joint retention.

E. Native feedback and conditional full calls
StreamCorrect supplies a concrete recursive feedback task
[1]. For complete closed state P and accepted audio X, expose
its recognition boundary as
A(P, X) = (B, M ),

(4)

where B contains ordered beam candidates and M the engine
state and inputs needed by original Correct/Update. Candidate
order, prompts, returned token IDs, selection, and update rules
remain intact.
Before learned correction finishes, the original update tail
with a typed first-beam identity prediction produces provisional
closed state Pb+ . It is a dependency projection, not a complete
selected result. A second worker can run the next complete
original call on that conditional input; both GPUs host the
full recognizer–corrector. Its own prediction may be wrong
even when its input later matches. Original Correct/Update
determines the complete result.
When the parent installs, the Owner compares the call’s
complete frozen start state, actual frontier, contract, and session
epoch with it. Equality confirms the unchanged owned call: it
continues if running, or becomes eligible for ordered selection
if complete. Job ID, birth, producer incarnation, inputs, and
execution history stay unchanged.
An earlier ancestor mismatch can remove current view
support without refuting a later complete input. Cancellation
before correction requires a disagreeing actual selected state
at that call’s own start frontier, coverage of its target without
remaining use, or a changed epoch. An unresolved future parent
supplies none of these proofs. The view can therefore require
withdrawal while the call remains unresolved.
This native input witness and the core Qwen boundarynode/unique-successor guard are different sufficient implementations of the continuation relation. Neither grants write
authority. Both use a whole-view Consumer that keeps certified
and provisional provenance separate. The native comparison
holds two complete workers fixed and varies conditional
dispatch and retention; ready-only ASR handoff is a separate
developmental control. For a call j, its complete frozen input
is confirmed when it matches the installed parent, refuted when
an actual selected state at its start frontier differs, and unknown
without either witness. Covered targets and fenced sessions
cannot supply selected results. Table I separates these input
decisions from actual view application.
The dispatcher scans available workers and sessions in their
existing order. Within each session it tries missing selected
work before a conditional tail, subject to the ahead bound;
it imposes no global selected-work priority across sessions.
Unknown input grants neither selection nor a refutation-based
cancellation proof. Replacing a typed prediction with its
complete result keeps only the suffix that consumes the actual
output contiguously. Physical release and producer-failure
handling follow Section IV.

F. [AU] Preserving the selected program
Let P ⇒A,X P ′ denote a transition allowed by the original
protected program on accepted immutable input X. The
relation may be nondeterministic. A complete original call
includes its original selection and update, or its declared
buffer-only path; a typed identity projection is not such a
result. We project a protocol execution onto its distinct durable
selected facts, deleting speculation, waiting, local rejection,
cancellation, collection, and repeated delivery. This restricted
projection follows the standard stuttering view of refinement [7];
provisional whole-view behavior remains outside its alphabet
and has a separate compatibility property.
Proposition 3 (Selected-trace refinement). Assume sufficient
declared state, faithful execution of frozen inputs, exclusive
serial selection, correctly bound message identities, and storage
that honors successful synchronization. If each new durable
fact binds a complete original call whose full input equals the
preceding selected cut, retains the call’s original identity, was
admitted under valid message authority, and is synchronized
before installation or certified application, then the projected
facts form an execution allowed by A. Replaying an existing
fact adds no transition.
Proof. The initial cut belongs to A. Deleted actions do not
extend the prefix. Each new fact binds a complete original
call on exactly the preceding cut and accepted input, hence an
allowed next edge regardless of dispatch time. Synchronization
precedes privileged application. Recovery replays the same
checked identities, including a fact first consumed after an
Owner crash, without adding an edge. Induction proves the
claim.
The durable append is the selection boundary; installation
and consumption do not select again. Already accepted buffers
retain their producer identities after exit, whereas new fenced

messages are inadmissible. The certified frontier derives only
from checked facts; ahead views remain provisional. Input
confirmation does not certify predicted next states.
A separately frozen specification explores 6,231 two-frontier
normal states and 14,074 one-frontier failure states, each to
a 12-event depth, with 27 named controls. An independent
trace checker binds actual facts and whole-view applications to
original call receipts. The specification is not extracted from the
runtime; depth bounds truncate exploration, and neither check
verifies all Python, CUDA, or operating-system behavior. Exact
equality with a fixed neural reference is separate empirical
evidence, rather than a premise that arbitrary GPU reruns must
be bitwise identical.
IV. E XECUTION P ROTOCOL
A. Complete results and physical ownership
In the core runtime, dispatch binds session, incarnation, job,
input, target, and contract. A row returns either a complete
result with re-derived state/text or cancellation without partial
state. Complete rows can be installed and consumed before
their batch finishes. Only all terminal rows and device synchronization authorize BatchDrained and executor reuse. The
native process lifecycle below has separate evidence.
B. Covered work and safe-point exit: K
For the tested use model, K requests exit when the installed
Canonical frontier covers a proposal’s target and it has no
remaining declared use. This differs from R: valid work
beyond the new root can remain useful. Workers observe
CancelRequest at decode boundaries; completion can win,
but each row has one terminal outcome. Static batches are not
compacted or refilled; masked costs and drain delays remain
measured.
C. Durable facts and outstanding input
Before acknowledgment, synchronized frames bind parent/next states, request identity, selected complete result, and
receipt; recorded deltas must reconstruct the state. Groupready commit shares synchronization among validated sessions
without a collection timeout or cross-session atomic transaction.
Uncertain append/application outcomes block writes. Writeahead recovery [8] reconstructs facts and identities; the native
extension binds this to original correction calls.
D. Validation boundaries
Propositions 1 and 2 concern the stated guards and compatible applications. The artifact retains an earlier, separately frozen
recovery prototype and its bounded model and crash results;
they do not validate the later core or native fault paths. The
selected-trace argument of Proposition 3, its finite specification,
and actual execution checks have distinct scopes.

Each job has an Owner namespace and monotonic issue
number; each physical slot has a worker incarnation, using
established RPC identity principles [9]. Fencing removes future
message authority without renaming already accepted complete
results.
