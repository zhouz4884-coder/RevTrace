## Required implementation, not merely a CPU proposal

1. Stable per-target identity(session,epoch,Parent version/digest,audio
   start/end,execution contract), with persistent shadow service obligations.
   Only an actual dispatch may create/update completion-suffix promises.
   Identity change/recovery invalidates unexecuted promises; they never become
   state authorization. Bounded service prevents repeated suffix replacement.
2. Released successor obligation chains, audio/deadline weights and actual
   remaining service estimates. Inherit successor deadlines into mandatory
   Canonical predecessors. No unreleased audio or guessed future token becomes
   a concrete execution job. At most two released successor layers initially.
3. Current and successor legal Private-display paths plus Canonical paths.
   Actual ACKs remain the source of observed credit. Pending row completion is
   predicted from real frozen job/lease age, not from arbitrary descendant age.
4. Actual known-if-verified target render and the existing exact-chain
   compatibility predicate. Forecast branches never bypass actual FP32
   verification/Generate, Trusted Render, admission checks or durable install.
   Unknown Direct/Fallback P is not automatic incompatibility; shared empirical
   compatibility estimates and explicit sparse uncertainty are required.
5. Bounded complete-cut planning with actual past Q/T, released successor value,
   full physical batch/fallback/Host delivery cost and real lease separation.
   A model-conditional empirical P90 promise is not a hard Commit-SLO guarantee.
   Do not restore unconditional Commit-first or discard zero-own-credit work.
6. GPU0 action selection may use real bounded subsets at Batch-drained, never
   merely label a permutation as joint-resource scheduling. Shared executor
   capabilities and simple ordering/subbatch controls must remain available to
   Fine/Standard. If no valid incremental action is implemented, state the
   precise limit rather than calling the algorithm complete.
