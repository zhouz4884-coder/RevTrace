\paragraph{Evidence and comparison scope}
Native confirmation uses one 8--9-s utterance per speaker; timing repeats
do not add acoustic sources. Native and core policy comparisons isolate
their respective retention effects, rather than rank external
speculative-serving systems.

\paragraph{Transferring the contract}
A new adapter must expose every mutable field affecting inference,
selection or feedback, including numerical caches, alignment cursors and
external state. Audio-frontier progress, new text and human accuracy
remain separate endpoints. Retention still pays synchronization,
queueing and inference; resource admission governs when to launch more
work~\cite{welsh2001seda}. Cold Owner recovery restores old facts before
new inference under the surviving components of
Section~\ref{subsec:failure_model}.

\paragraph{Reproducibility}
The anonymous material supplies source snapshots, fixed model/data
configurations, per-run identities, endpoint-checking scripts and
hash-bound figure inputs. Bounded summaries and raw-log hash indices are
included; weights and full journals are separate. Figure programs
regenerate plots from the frozen inputs.
