\paragraph{Guarantee and adapter scope}
A new adapter must expose every mutable field affecting inference,
selection or feedback, including numerical caches, alignment cursors and
external state. The witnesses permit conservative rejection when this
evidence is absent. Dependency validity, selected-model text agreement
and human accuracy remain distinct; compatible retention also permits
later legitimate revision. Bounded specifications and observed traces
check separate parts of this contract.

\paragraph{Service and capacity}
Advancing the audio frontier can leave cumulative text unchanged.
The original-clock coverage, text-occurrence counts and quality metrics
therefore remain separate. Retention does not eliminate synchronization,
queueing or neural work. The c12/c16 results expose timing limits. An
earlier saturated two-source comparison does not beat independent full
replicas under its separate warmup/Consumer contract. Single-chain gains
do not establish saturated capacity.
Resident independent EOS requests also do not repair the original
no-reset stream's quality and deadline failures. Cold Owner recovery
restores old facts before it supplies new inference, under the surviving
components of Section~\ref{subsec:failure_model}.

Input eligibility permits retention; queue-based resource admission
governs when to launch more work~\cite{welsh2001seda}.
