\subsection{Observed protocol-path costs}
\label{subsec:protocol_cost}
We replay four actual source/policy traces in 12 fresh CPU processes and
run 16 normal native trials: two fixed sources, two policies, recording
off/on and two fresh repeats. Replays reproduce the complete fact-journal
bytes and Consumer semantics. All eight off/on pairs preserve the selected
states, original corrections and returned IDs.

Table~\ref{tab:protocol_cost} reports the eight trials with timing enabled.
Synchronous storage accounts for most durable-commit wall time. Service
comparisons include these complete-state validation, persistence and
whole-view processing costs.
