\subsection{Preserving acquired results through process failures}
\label{subsec:same_source_validation}
The final coordinator preserves acquired results without granting old
messages new authority. Both policies share models, durability, guards,
recovery and Consumer. Initial loading and fixed warmup precede service;
replacement loading, replay, recomputation and physical drain retain the
original clock.

Six conditions, two policies and two fresh processes on one development
clip give 24 short trials, plus four EOS/compatible-view regressions.
Together with eight sustained-state trials below, these 36 trials contain
3,976 durable transitions independently bound to complete original call
receipts and actual Consumer applications, with zero illegal installations
or incorrect owned-message rejections. Eight duplicate/late messages cause
no selected-state mutation; eight worker deaths have confirmed physical
replacements. Conditional calls supply 354 durable facts: 352 have original
Owner installation records, and two first reach the Consumer through
recovery. In four worker-after-ASR cases, a previously consumed whole view
remains across producer exit/fencing until legitimate correction. Its
support, rather than the producer's continued presence, determines retention.

Worker and Owner failures preserve legality but have different service

Old durable facts reappear in 1.36--1.81~s, whereas new certified

worker can finish a call before replacement readiness, so replay, new
results and replacement loading have separate recovery clocks.

\paragraph{Complete buffers across producer death}
Two matching and two mismatching timing controls isolate a complete result
already registered by the Owner. The fixture holds the parent after
original ASR and the child after sending its complete original result.
We terminate the producer, verify exit, CUDA release and sealed-channel
validation, fence new messages, and release original parent correction.
Both matching results install and are consumed once with their original
job, birth and incarnation; both mismatches are rejected against the
actual same-frontier parent. Each positive case also completes a subsequent
independent request. These controlled interleavings establish result
retention and rejection, rather than fault-time SLOs.
\par\noindent\textbf{Finding 4: producer failure and result invalidity are different.}
Producer exit revokes future message authority. An acquired result retains
its original identity and is checked against its actual parent.

\paragraph{Sustained-state stress and its limits}
Two metadata-selected AISHELL-1 speakers supply 24 utterances each,
concatenated as exact PCM into 228.51025/208.1003125-s streams without
resets. A continuous original-engine reference and 875 restored steps
agree on complete state, correction and IDs across 398/357 buffer shifts
and 353/252 context changes. Eight serving trials produce 3,500 matching
selected steps. Unpinned terminal and audit windows stay at most 64
entries; live dependencies, state/output bytes, RSS and cumulative logs
are separate.

All eight trials fail D2/C8 and reproduce severe original-task insertion
errors, with higher energy for RevTrace. They validate state capture and
collection through long executions, rather than sustained ASR service;
the artifact reports the complete latency, quality and resource endpoints.
Across all 36 trials, the checker preserves 90 compatible-ahead
dependency-tip applications and records 1,118 necessary incompatible
withdrawals. These counts concern dependency tips, rather than new text.

Input confirmation and use have different denominators. The dispatch
audit confirms 40/57 conditional inputs on normal short traces, 58/90
under controlled faults, and 96/924 and 166/830 on the two no-reset sources;
none remain unresolved. Six confirmed jobs do not become durable under
injected failures. The fault stratum supplies 52 durable, consumed results:
50 have original-Owner installation records and two first reach the
Consumer through recovery.
