\title{RevTrace: Preserving Valid Progress across Trusted Feedback Revisions}
\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous submission}\IEEEauthorblockA{Regular paper}}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Feedback-driven inference may withdraw a still-supported view even after
timely delivery. RevTrace addresses these avoidable withdrawals by
coordinating complete-input eligibility, original selection authority,
and whole-view support. It retains eligible calls without endorsing their
predictions and preserves matching received results across producer
failure. We give sufficient retention conditions and a conditional
selected-trace refinement argument. In a 24-speaker factorial study,
computation retention increases frontier withdrawals from 12 to 17;
joint retention yields zero in the preselected setting. In a separate
native-correction study on eight previously unused speakers,
dual-deadline audio coverage rises from 40.6\% to 93.9\% over conservative
execution, with unchanged selected histories and final quality.
The speaker-paired gain is 53.3 percentage points (95\% CI [44.8, 59.6]);
every source improves. Whole-run passes increase from 0/16 to 10/16.
Controlled failures validate retention of matching received results and
rejection of mismatches.
\end{abstract}
