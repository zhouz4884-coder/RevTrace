\subsection{One revision, three different decisions}
Figure~\ref{fig:protocol} follows call $j_3$, started on provisional
$\widehat P_2$ before its parent's original Correct/Update selects $P_2$.
A full-input match at $P_2$ confirms [EL], preserving $j_3$'s identity.
Its own prediction $\widehat P_3$ may still
differ from the original selected result $P_3$: [AU] selects that complete
result only after its actual parent is installed.
Independently, installing $P_2$ checks [SUP] on the current whole view
$h$. Losing $h$'s support does not refute a later call's unknown input.
Table~\ref{tab:decision_objects} separates the three objects and uses.
Subscripts denote states; each call freezes all contract inputs.
In the evaluation, $R/S$ switch retention actions under these judgments.
