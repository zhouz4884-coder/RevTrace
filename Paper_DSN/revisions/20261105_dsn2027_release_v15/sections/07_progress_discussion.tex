RevTrace coordinates permission to reuse a computation with support for
the view currently delivered. This keeps a trusted revision from
unnecessarily discarding work that still has a valid use.

\paragraph{Evidence and comparison scope}
Native confirmation uses one 8--9-s utterance per speaker; timing repeats
do not add acoustic sources. Native and core policy comparisons isolate
their respective retention effects, rather than rank external
speculative-serving systems.

\paragraph{Transferring the contract}
An adapter must (1) capture and restore all state affecting later
inference, selection or feedback; (2) bind complete original results,
selection/update, identities and durable transitions; and (3) register
the actual Consumer's whole-view provenance and dependencies for retention
or withdrawal. Section~\ref{sec:contract} instantiates these obligations
for Qwen feedback tokens and native buffer/context/alignment state.
Hidden caches, external state or irreversible effects require additional
adapter support.
Retention still pays synchronization, queueing and inference; resource
admission governs when to launch more work~\cite{welsh2001seda}.
Cold Owner recovery uses the surviving components of
Section~\ref{subsec:failure_model}.

\paragraph{Reproducibility}
The anonymous companion rebuilds summaries, paired confidence intervals and figures
from bounded records. It includes source snapshots, frozen configurations,
per-run identities and endpoint-checking scripts. Full-journal rechecking
and model reruns additionally require the indexed journals, recordings,
weights and dependencies; a hash index alone does not recheck their content.
